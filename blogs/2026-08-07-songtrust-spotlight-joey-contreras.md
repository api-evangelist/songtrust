JOEY CONTRERAS is a New York–based Mexican-American musical theatre and pop songwriter whose work has garnered over 75 million streams across music and social media platforms. He is a two-time Fred Ebb Award finalist, three-time Jonathan Larson Grant finalist, and an alum of the Johnny Mercer Songwriters Project. Most recently, his musical In Pieces (in development with Regular People) played a sold-out run of performances at The Other Palace in London, UK.
