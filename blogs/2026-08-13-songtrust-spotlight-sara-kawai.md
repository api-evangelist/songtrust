SARA KAWAI is a versatile LA-based harpist and producer with 18 years of studio/live experience playing the harp and 8 years in music production. Hailing from Boston, Sara began her journey with classical harp training and is now blending her classical roots with the dynamic worlds of hip-hop, R&B, alternative, pop, electronic, and more. Sara has performed with a diverse range of artists, including SZA, Kehlani, Lizzo, Blxst, The Marias, 03 Greedo, Pink Sweat$, Mereba, Kalan Frfr, Sabrina Claudio and more, most recently on BET Awards Show with Kehlani, Jimmy Kimmel!
